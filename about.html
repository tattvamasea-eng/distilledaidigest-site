Why D·A·D Exists

The Problem We're Solving

The AI news cycle never stops. New models, new vendors, new claims — and a stack of newsletters racing to be first.

Almost none of it answers the only question that matters on Monday: what does this change for my shop, and what should I do?

Distilled AI Digest exists to answer that. Every Sunday we cut the week to five developments, with the context a CIO needs to brief a board, a budget, or a team.

How We Choose

One filter: would a senior technology leader need this to do the job well next week?

We read the primary sources — lab notes, filings, earnings, incident write-ups — then say what it means in plain language. We do not chase clicks. We do not sell certainty we do not have. We do not write a headline we cannot stand behind three months later.

Each issue also carries a CIO Corner (what to do) and The Stack (energy, chips, cloud, models, apps). No paywall. The archive is open.

Who Writes It

Subramanian Ranganathan (Ram) writes Distilled AI Digest. He is an enterprise technology partner who got tired of spending a Sunday reconstructing the week from twenty tabs.

The byline on the issue is The Distilled AI Digest Team. Day-to-day, follow the work at x.com/BramForge.
